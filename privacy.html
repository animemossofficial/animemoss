Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information AnimeMOSS may collect, why it may be used, and the choices available to users when using the AnimeMOSS website and related services.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, AnimeMOSS may process information such as your username, display name, email address, account authentication information, comments, and other information you voluntarily provide.

The service may also process technical and security information such as IP addresses, timestamps, browser or device information, request information, and security logs where reasonably necessary to operate and protect the service.

2. How information is used

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To authenticate users and maintain sessions.
  • To provide email verification and password-reset functionality.
  • To provide comments and other requested website features.
  • To prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and security attacks.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Cookies and sessions

AnimeMOSS may use cookies or similar technologies for authentication, session management, security, preferences, and other essential functionality.

4. Comments and public information

Comments and other information intentionally submitted to public areas of the website may be visible to other visitors. Users should avoid publishing personal information they do not want to make public.

5. Third-party services

AnimeMOSS may rely on third-party services for functions such as authentication, email delivery, anime metadata, hosting, analytics, or other infrastructure. Information processed by those services may also be subject to their own policies and terms.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with applicable obligations, and support legitimate operational needs.

8. Account and privacy requests

Users may contact AnimeMOSS regarding account or privacy questions through the official Contact page. Requests may be subject to reasonable verification and applicable legal requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service, its practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to animemossofficial@gmail.com.
